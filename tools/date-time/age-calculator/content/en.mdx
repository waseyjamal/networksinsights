Age Calculator works out an exact age from a date of birth, in years, months and days, and shows the total days lived and the date of the next birthday. It suits anyone filling in a form, planning a birthday, or checking how old someone will be on a given day. The second date is today by default, and you can change it to see an age in the past or the future.

## How to use

1. Pick the **Date of birth** with the date field, or type it in your browser's date format.
2. Leave **Age on** as it is to measure the age today, using the calendar of your device, or pick another date to measure on.
3. Read the four cells: **Years**, **Months**, **Days** and **Total days**. The first three add up to the exact age, and the last counts every day between the two dates.
4. Under them, a sentence names the next birthday with its weekday, how many days away it is, and the age being reached.
5. If a date is missing, or the birth date comes after the age-on date, a message says so.

## Examples

Born on 15 May 1990 and measured on 2 October 2026, the age is 36 years, 4 months and 17 days, which is 13,289 days in all. The next birthday is Saturday, 15 May 2027, 225 days later, turning 37.

For a birth on 29 February 2000, measured on 28 February 2001, the age is exactly 1 year. In a year that is not a leap year, the birthday is counted on 28 February, and the next birthday after 2 October 2026 is Sunday, 28 February 2027.

## Limits

It uses the Gregorian calendar for every date, even before that calendar was adopted, and accepts years from 1 to 9999. The age is calendar based: when the birth day does not exist in a month, the last day of that month is used, so a 29 February birthday falls on 28 February in a common year. Some legal systems count it on 1 March instead. The time of birth and time zones are not used.

## FAQ

### How is the age counted when I was born on 29 February?

In a year that is not a leap year, the birthday is treated as 28 February, and in a leap year it is 29 February. Some countries use 1 March for legal purposes, so check the rule that applies to you.

### What does the age-on date do?

It sets the day the age is measured on. Leave it as today for a current age, or choose a future date to see how old someone will be then, or a past date for an age at that time.

### Why does it refuse a birth date in the future?

An age cannot be negative, so a birth date after the age-on date has no result. The message shows both dates. Change either one, and the age appears at once.

### Is my date of birth sent anywhere?

No. Both dates stay in this page on your device, and the calculation runs there without any upload.
