Remove Duplicate Lines deletes repeated lines from a list and tells you how many it removed, with options to ignore case, trim spaces, drop empty lines and sort. It helps anyone cleaning an email list, a column pasted from a spreadsheet, a keyword list or a log, where the same entry appears more than once. The result updates as you type or change an option.

## How to use

1. Paste or type your list into **Your lines**, one item per line.
2. Tick **Ignore case** to treat Apple and apple as the same line.
3. Tick **Trim spaces** to ignore spaces and tabs at the start and end of each line; the result is trimmed too.
4. Tick **Remove empty lines** to drop blank lines altogether.
5. Under **Keep**, choose whether the first or the last copy of a repeated line stays, and under **Sort**, keep the original order or sort A to Z or Z to A.
6. Read the counts of lines before, after and removed, then press **Copy result**.

## Limits

The text may be up to 1,000,000 characters, about 1 MB of plain English; that limit is our own choice, and a text of that size is processed without freezing the page. Lines are split at LF or CRLF, and the result uses LF. A line break at the very end does not count as another line. With **Remove empty lines** but not **Trim spaces**, a line holding only spaces is not empty. Sorting follows English alphabetical order, so it may not match the order another language expects.

## FAQ

### Which copy stays when I choose the last one?

The last occurrence of each line is kept, and it stays in the position where it appeared in your list, so earlier copies are the ones taken out.

### Does ignoring case change my text?

No. It only decides which lines count as the same. The line that is kept keeps its original capital letters.

### Is my list sent anywhere?

No. The lines are compared in this page on your device, and nothing is uploaded or stored.
