# 0017. License policy for code, fonts and AI models

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

The site must be usable commercially.

## Decision

No AGPL, GPL, SSPL or non-commercial licenses, for code, fonts or AI models, without an owner-approved ADR.

## Consequences

Every new dependency needs a license check in its plan.

## Revisit when

No trigger set.
