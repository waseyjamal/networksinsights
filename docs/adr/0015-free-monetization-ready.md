# 0015. Free at launch, monetization-ready

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

The site is free at launch, but a paid tier may come later.

## Decision

Keep the site free at launch. Reserve layout zones and include a limits field in the tool manifest.

## Consequences

Limits are unlimited at launch. A future Pro tier can use the field without a redesign.

## Revisit when

No trigger set.
