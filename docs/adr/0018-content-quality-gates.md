# 0018. Build-time content quality gates

Status: Superseded by [0036](0036-content-quality-gates.md)
Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

At 500+ tools, thin or duplicate pages would hurt quality.

## Decision

Enforce content quality at build time: required content sections and no near-duplicate content.

## Consequences

A tool with thin or duplicated content fails the build.

## Revisit when

No trigger set.
