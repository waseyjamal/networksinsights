Turn a PNG picture into a JPG file without leaving the page. The conversion runs in your browser, so the picture is never uploaded anywhere. It suits anyone who needs a lossless copy of a photo or screenshot to edit, or a format that a design tool or a form insists on.

## How to use

Drag a PNG file onto the drop area, or choose one from your device. The tool decodes the picture and draws it on a canvas at its full pixel size. Press Convert to encode the canvas as JPG, then use Download to save the new file. Converting several pictures is the same routine, one after another. The saved file keeps the original name with a new ending.

## Examples

A PNG screenshot of a settings page, saved from a chat app, comes out as a JPG with sharp text edges and the same width and height. A holiday photo works the same way, but the JPG will be several times larger than the PNG, because JPG stores every pixel without discarding detail.

## Limits

The output is only as good as the input: PNG compression artifacts that are already in the picture stay in the JPG. Converting does not restore lost detail or add transparency. Camera details such as the shooting date and location are not carried over. Very large pictures may fail on phones with little memory.

## FAQ

### Is my picture uploaded?

No. The picture is read and converted inside your browser tab, and nothing is sent to a server.

### Why is the JPG so much bigger than the PNG?

JPG keeps every pixel exactly, while PNG throws away detail to save space. For photographs the difference is large, and for flat graphics it is small.

### Will the JPG have a transparent background?

No. A PNG has no transparency, so there is nothing to keep. The converted picture keeps the colors it had.
