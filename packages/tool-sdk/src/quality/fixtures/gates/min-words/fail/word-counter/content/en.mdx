Counts words.

## How to use

Type text and read the number.

## Examples

The sentence "The quick brown fox" has four words. A hyphenated word such as "well-known" counts as one word, and a number such as 2026 counts as one word too. A line of three spaces between two words still counts as two words.

## Limits

It may differ from other counters.

## FAQ

### Is the text I paste stored anywhere?

No. The counting happens in your browser, and the text is not sent to a server or saved.

### Are numbers and symbols counted as words?

Numbers are, because they are read as words. A symbol standing alone, such as an ampersand between spaces, is not.

### Does it need an internet connection?

Only to load the page. After that the counting works offline, because it happens in your browser.
