Count the words in any text as you type or paste it. The count updates on every keystroke and nothing you write leaves the page. Use it for essays with a word limit, articles with a target length, or product descriptions that have to fit a box.

## How to use

Type into the text box or paste what you already have. The word total appears above the box and changes as the text changes. Select part of the text to count only the selection, which helps when a limit applies to one section such as an abstract. Clear the box with the Clear button to start again.

## Examples

The sentence "The quick brown fox" has four words. A hyphenated word such as "well-known" counts as one word, and a number such as 2026 counts as one word too. A line of three spaces between two words still counts as two words.

## Limits

Different programs count differently. A word processor may treat a dash or a slash between two words another way, so a school or a publisher that uses its own counter may show a slightly different total. Languages written without spaces between words, such as Chinese, cannot be counted this way.

## FAQ

### Is the text I paste stored anywhere?

No. The counting happens in your browser, and the text is not sent to a server or saved.

### Are numbers and symbols counted as words?

Numbers are, because they are read as words. A symbol standing alone, such as an ampersand between spaces, is not.

### Does it need an internet connection?

Only to load the page. After that the counting works offline, because it happens in your browser.
