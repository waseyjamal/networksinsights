Join several PDF files into one document, in the order you choose. The merge happens on your device, so contracts, invoices and scans are never uploaded. It helps when you must send a single attachment, or want a chapter, a report and an appendix to read as one.

## How to use

Add your PDF files by dropping them in or by browsing. Each file appears as a row that you can drag up or down to set the order. Remove a file with the cross beside it. When the list is right, press Merge, and then save the combined document with Download.

## Examples

Three files named cover, report and appendix, added in that order, become one PDF that opens on the cover and ends with the last page of the appendix. Dragging the appendix to the top first puts its pages at the start instead.

## Limits

A PDF that is locked with a password cannot be merged until the password is removed. The combined file is roughly the sum of its parts, so it may be too big for email. Form fields and bookmarks from the source files may not survive the merge, and very large files can use a lot of memory.

## FAQ

### Are my PDF files sent to a server?

No. The files are read and joined inside your browser, and they never leave your device.

### Does merging change how the pages look?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text, images and page sizes stay the same.

### Is there a limit on how many files I can add?

The limit is your browser memory, not the tool. If the page slows down, merge in two rounds instead of one.
