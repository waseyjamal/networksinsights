Shrink a PNG into a JPG when the file is too heavy to send or post. The work happens on your device, in this tab, and the picture goes nowhere else. Choose it for photographs and large screenshots, where a slightly smaller, slightly softer file is a fair trade for a much lighter one.

## How to use

Add your PNG by dropping it into the box or by browsing for it. Set the quality with the slider: a higher number keeps more detail and makes a bigger file, a lower number does the opposite. Press Convert, look at the size shown next to the preview, and save the result with Download. If the size is still too large, lower the quality and convert again.

## Examples

A PNG photo taken from a phone screen may drop to a fraction of its size at a middle quality setting, with no change you can spot at normal viewing size. A logo with a see-through background becomes a JPG with a plain white background behind it.

## Limits

JPG cannot store transparency, so any see-through area is filled with white. Each conversion at lower quality loses detail for good, so keep the original PNG. Sharp lines and small text can show blurry edges at low quality. Animated PNG files are saved as a single still frame.

## FAQ

### Does the picture leave my computer?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, and no copy of the picture is kept or sent.

### What quality setting should I pick?

Start in the middle and look at the preview. Raise it if you see blocky patches, and lower it if the file is still bigger than you need.

### Can I get the transparency back later?

No. Once the picture is saved as a JPG the see-through pixels are gone, so convert a copy and keep the PNG.
