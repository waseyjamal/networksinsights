Check how many characters a piece of text holds, with and without spaces. It answers to the limits set by social posts, search snippets, text messages and form fields. Everything is counted in your browser while you type, so private drafts stay private.

## How to use

Paste or type the text into the box. Two numbers appear straight away: all characters, and characters without spaces. Edit the text until the number you care about is under the limit you were given. Copy the shortened text back out when you are done, or press Clear to begin a new one.

## Examples

The word "hello" is five characters. The phrase "hello there" is eleven characters with the space and ten without it. A line break in the middle of a paragraph is counted as one character, the same as a space, so a tweet-sized note with two short lines is a little longer than it looks.

## Limits

An emoji or an accented letter made of several parts can be counted as more than one character by some platforms, so a service with a strict limit may disagree with this total by a few. The tool measures what you see, not how a particular website stores it. Long texts of a million characters may slow down older phones.

## FAQ

### Do spaces count as characters?

Yes, in the first number. The second number leaves them out, for the places that count only visible letters.

### Why does my platform show a different total?

Platforms count emoji, links and line breaks by their own rules. Treat this total as a close guide and leave a small margin.

### Can I count a whole document?

Yes. Paste the full text, or select a part of it to count only that part. Very long texts work, but may feel slower on older devices.
