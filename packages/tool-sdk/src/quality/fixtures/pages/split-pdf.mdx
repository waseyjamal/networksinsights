Pull the pages you need out of a long PDF and leave the rest behind. Choose a range, or cut the document into pieces, and save the results as new files. The document is opened locally in your browser, which matters when it holds anything private.

## How to use

Open a PDF by dropping it on the page. A row of small page previews appears. Type a range such as 3-5 or a list such as 1, 4, 9, or click the previews you want. Pick whether the chosen pages go into one file, or each range into its own file, then press Split and download what it makes.

## Examples

Entering 2-3 from a ten-page contract gives a new two-page PDF that holds only the signature pages. Asking for a new file every four pages turns a twelve-page report into three separate documents, named so that they sort in page order when you save them.

## Limits

Password-protected files must be unlocked first. Links that point to a page you did not keep will stop working in the new file. Pages that are very heavy with images make the previews slow to appear, and files above what your browser can hold in memory will fail to open.

## FAQ

### Does splitting delete pages from my original?

No. Your original file is left as it was, and the new files are separate copies.

### Can I put the pages in a different order?

Not here. This tool selects and separates pages. Reordering belongs to a different tool.

### Will the text stay selectable in the new files?

Yes. Pages are copied as they are, so text that could be selected in the original can be selected in the pieces.

### Can I split a scanned document?

Yes, because a scan is a set of page pictures. Splitting works the same way, though the files may be large.
